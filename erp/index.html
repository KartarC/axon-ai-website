Who it is for

Built around your kind of work.

CNC and precision job shops

Manage changing priorities, repeat parts and customer-specific instructions. Keep the job number and drawing revision clear as work moves between machines.

Laser and sheet-metal shops

Bring Han’s LaserNest exports into the quoting pilot. Apply your material, machine, gas and electricity rates, then review a customer-facing PDF before issuing it.

Small manufacturing teams

Replace disconnected handoffs one process at a time. Start with a production board, costing or digital travelers instead of assuming every workflow must change at once.

How it fits

A clear workflow, from start to finish.

Estimate and review

Capture the part, quantity, material and revision. The quoting pilot supports staff-imported Han’s reports; it does not automatically price arbitrary customer drawings.

Plan and execute

Use the production board to organize work across machines and digital travelers to communicate instructions, checks and sign-offs.

Review and improve

Compare quoted costs with recorded labor and material costs. Decide what to adjust for the next job based on the records your team maintains.

Questions about Ovrendi ERP

Does Ovrendi replace accounting or CAD/CAM software?

Those integrations and replacements are not included in the current live tools. Keep your existing accounting and CAD/CAM workflows, and discuss any required connection before choosing a rollout.

Is inventory available today?

Material and remnant inventory, maintenance, customer portal, certificates and outside-service modules remain in development. Production, job costing and digital travelers are live; laser quoting is a pilot.

How should a job shop evaluate the trial?

Use a representative job with known quantities and costs. Check the workflow with an estimator and operator, and compare the output with your existing process before relying on it for customer work.
