Service terms — draft for review

Updated October 9, 2026.

Draft — not final contractual terms.

The operator’s legal name, mailing address and final commercial provisions require confirmation and legal review. This page is not presented as an executed agreement. Any signed pilot or customer agreement remains controlling.

Service and authorized use

Ovrendi provides the modules enabled for your company workspace. Product pages distinguish available tools from pilots and features in development. Roadmap descriptions are not delivery commitments. Use the service for lawful business activities, protect login credentials, and upload only information you are authorized to provide. Do not interfere with other workspaces, probe unauthorized access or misuse the service.

Your records and quoting responsibility

You retain ownership of your business records. Ovrendi needs permission to host and process those records to deliver the service. Company administrators manage users and access. Imported quantities, material details, machine rates, gas consumption, electricity costs, taxes and commercial terms must be reviewed before issuing a quotation. Estimates are not machine programming, engineering advice or a guarantee of actual production cost. PDF quotes omit internal rates and margins.

Trials, subscriptions and currency

Standard signup trials are 14 days without a card unless a separate pilot period is agreed. No-card trials do not automatically charge a payment method. When you explicitly complete subscription checkout, the USD price, billing frequency and first charge date shown there apply, including any remaining trial. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Payment requires a working live checkout; test-mode transactions do not charge real money.

Use Manage billing to review or cancel a subscription before the next renewal. A scheduled cancellation normally ends access at the displayed billing-period end. Failed payments may restrict module access. Final tax, refund, price-change notice and suspension provisions must be confirmed before commercial launch. Existing agreed customer pricing is not changed by this draft.

Support, availability and data

Use Report a bug to submit issues, screenshots and replies. No guaranteed response time or uptime commitment is included unless separately agreed in writing. Read the Privacy and data practices notice for data use, providers, account activity, analytics choices and requests. Request data export before closing a workspace.

Items requiring legal completion

Registered operator identity; governing law and dispute process; warranty and liability provisions; refunds and taxes; termination, export and deletion timelines; update and price-change notice; customer data-processing terms and provider obligations. These items must be reviewed by a qualified adviser rather than inferred from a software template.
