Production & job tracking

Learn the production workflow when this module is enabled for your company.

Updated October 18, 2026 · Based on the current Ovrendi workflow

In this tutorial

Open Quoting

Check access

Production Board must be enabled for your company. A Quoting-only trial does not include it. Owners, admins and managers can convert quotes and assign work. Operators can update stages, instructions and usage. Viewers can read job progress.

Turn an accepted quote into a job

  1. Open the saved quotation in Quoting and select Review.
  2. Issue it and collect customer acceptance through the private approval link, or record acceptance after receiving it separately.
  3. Refresh responses, then select Create / open production job.

The accepted revision supplies the customer name, material, part dimensions, quantities and quoted price. Repeating the action opens the same job. One accepted quote batch creates one job. A matching customer record is linked only when its name is unambiguous.

Plan the work

Open a job card. Assign a machine, due date and priority. Add drawing references, secondary operations and inspection instructions. Machine choices come from your company’s machine list; quoting cost profiles are separate.

For work without a quotation, use Operation board / new job. These jobs also appear in job tracking.

Track the batch

Save the job’s stage as work progresses: Planned → Cutting → Secondary operations → Inspection → Ready for pickup → Completed. Use On hold or Cancelled when needed. Completed represents fulfilled work. Stages are staff-recorded; they do not certify inspection or automatically check traveler sign-offs.

The operation board tracks individual operations separately. Changing a job stage does not automatically complete its operation cards. Use Shop Travelers, if enabled, for existing instructions and sign-off workflows.

Record actual usage

Enter cumulative actual hours and material used, choosing sheets, kg or pieces. Managers can enter cumulative material and labour costs in the quotation’s currency. Blank means not yet recorded; zero means a confirmed zero.

Saved material and labour totals automatically feed Job Costing when that module is enabled. They replace other entries in the same categories to prevent double-counting; older entries remain visible as excluded. Outside operations and overhead remain additive. Blank production totals use existing cost entries; a confirmed zero overrides them. Hours are displayed separately and are not converted to money without a recorded labour total. The accepted quote supplies price, currency and estimated basis. Margins reflect costs recorded so far, not a verified final margin. Inventory is not deducted.

Save and review

Select Save job progress. Changes and stage history are saved to your company account. If another user updated the job first, reload the saved job and reapply your changes. Use search, machine and stage filters to find work. Refresh the board for the latest changes. The board shows up to 500 recent jobs; history shows the latest 50 updates per job.
