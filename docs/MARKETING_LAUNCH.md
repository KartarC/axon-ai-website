# Ovrendi Quoting: first 30-day marketing campaign

## Objective and audience
Generate qualified demo enquiries for the Quoting pilot among Canadian and US laser-cutting and fabrication shops. Start with owners, estimators and operations managers using supported Han’s exports. Initial planning targets: 5 qualified demo enquiries, 3 completed demos and 2 trial workspaces in 30 days. These are proposed targets, not forecasts or proven results.

## Offer and message
Your cutting report. A clearer quotation. Staff import a supported report, apply their shop’s rates, review a saved revision and share a PDF or approval link. Evidence: current Quoting workflow and Education guides. No claims of time savings, margin improvement, compliance certification, manufacturer endorsement or customer outcomes without evidence.

The pilot does not offer unattended drawing-to-price automation, automatic nesting or automatic quote-to-job creation. Customers supply their rates. A private approval link records a decision, not a payment. Do not advertise Individually agreed extended trials as a general offer.

## Channels and budget
Owned landing page and educational posts; reviewed outreach to contacts you are authorized to approach; demonstrations with synthetic or approved sample data. No paid budget has been approved, so no ads are activated. Assess paid search only after actual enquiries and demo feedback establish message and product fit. Email drafts require an approved business signature, recipient review and appropriate opt-out details before sending. No bulk send or mailing-list signup is part of the enquiry form.

## Weekly calendar

Publish post 2 and send approved follow-ups. Record common objections in opportunity notes.

Publish post 3 and demonstrate revision and approval handling.

Dependency: Use synthetic data unless customer permission is recorded.

### Week 4: Review and improve

Publish post 4. Review enquiries, demos, trials and first saved quotes; update the next month’s plan.

Dependency: Case studies require customer permission and measured results.

## Operating workflow
Demo requests use the existing validated contact endpoint and appear as ERP enquiries prefixed [Quoting demo enquiry] in the CRM. The form confirms saving, not a booked appointment or delivered email. Staff review the enquiry, prepare a Sales opportunity, assign owner/next action/date, and arrange the demo by email. After qualification, link the correct company and use onboarding tasks. Moving a sales stage does not provision access or enable billing.

## Measurement and review
Review weekly: consented landing-page visits, form starts and successful submissions; qualified opportunities; completed demos; trial workspaces; first saved quote; conversion to an agreed paid subscription. Website analytics uses channel categories and consent. Campaign query parameters are provided for future attribution, but current reports do not offer per-campaign revenue analysis. Paid sales labels are staff-entered; reconcile payments with Stripe before reporting revenue.

### Post 1 · The quoting workflow

A cutting report gives you production information. A customer quotation also needs your shop’s commercial decisions.

Ovrendi’s Quoting pilot brings those steps together: import a supported Han’s report, review the quantities, apply your rates, and save a quotation revision. Then share a PDF or a private approval link.

### Post 2 · Costing question

When you price a laser-cutting job, is gas included in your machine rate—or added separately?

The same question applies to electricity and labour. A detailed estimate can still double-count costs if the assumptions are unclear.

Our guide walks through the inputs to review before saving a quote:
https://getovrendi.com/education/understand-pricing/

### Post 3 · Approval workflow

A quotation is the start of a decision, not the end of the workflow.

Ovrendi lets you share a private link so a customer can accept, decline or request changes, with an optional purchase-order number. Staff can review that response alongside the saved quotation.

Approval records a decision; it does not collect payment.

See how the workflow works:
https://getovrendi.com/education/customer-approvals/

### Post 4 · Report review checklist

Before pricing a nest, check five things:

1. Material and thickness
2. Full-sheet dimensions
3. Requested versus nested quantity
4. Processing time and report warnings
5. Currency and the basis of the material price

A saved estimate is only as useful as the inputs you reviewed. Here is our Han’s report checklist:
https://getovrendi.com/education/import-review/

### Demo agenda

1. Ask about the shop’s current quoting process and the main difficulty.
2. Confirm the export format is supported. Use synthetic or approved sample data.
3. Import and compare material, dimensions and nested quantities.
4. Enter customer-provided rates; explain gas and electricity assumptions.
5. Calculate, review and save a revision.
6. Preview the customer document and demonstrate an approval link.
7. Agree on a trial success criterion and next review date.
8. Record owner, next action and due date in Sales pipeline. Do not promise roadmap features or automatic payments.
