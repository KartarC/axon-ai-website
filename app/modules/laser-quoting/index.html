Start here · Step-by-step quoting guide
  1. Import: Choose the Han’s report. Part sizes, quantities, stock size and processing times are read automatically. Review report warnings.
  2. Choose your profiles: Load customer defaults, then the machine, gas and electricity standards for this job. These are your own saved assumptions, not manufacturer-certified standards.
  3. Complete required fields: Check currency and material price. Use the small ? beside any field for help. Optional cost adjustments start at zero.
  4. Review: Calculate the estimate, resolve missing settings and tick the review confirmation.
  5. Save: Save a revision before closing. Find it under Saved quotations. Reopening and editing creates a new revision; previously saved prices stay unchanged.
  6. Share: Preview the saved revision, mark issued, then create a private approval link or download a PDF. Internal rates and margin are excluded from the customer document.

Quotes and profiles are saved to the connected database when you press Save. Working quote fields autosave after a short pause. Wait for “Working draft saved” before leaving. Recovered drafts must be recalculated and reviewed; they are not issued quotations.

3. Quote breakdown

How your estimate is calculated

Material uses full-sheet quantity and price, or sheet dimensions, thickness, density and price per kg. Any entered remnant credit is deducted.

Machine processing uses imported cutting, piercing and travel time. Setup and additional labor are added using the time and rates you enter.

Gas combines cutting and piercing flow with the corresponding report times, plus any extra gas-on time. Bottle-pack pricing is converted using its usable gas volume.

Electricity uses average electrical input, processing and setup time, and your price per kWh. This is an estimate, not a meter reading.

Rounded cost lines are added. Your margin or markup and minimum charge determine the selling price. Taxes are excluded. In Han’s comparison mode, report charges replace material, machine and labor calculations; check for double counting.

Import a report and enter your rates. Missing consumption values will not be treated as zero.
