Submit & review employee weeks

Submit your stopped sessions for review. If a manager returns your timesheet, read their comment, ask them to correct any hours, then resubmit. Changes to time entries require a new submission. Hourly rates and labour costs stay private.

Weekly approval & export

Monday–Sunday, UTC. Sessions belong to the week they started. Changes require approval again. Exports contain private session labour costs, not payroll or manual job-cost overrides.

Private employee hourly rates

Only owners, admins and managers can view these rates. Changes apply to new timers. Correct a completed entry separately to change its saved rate.
