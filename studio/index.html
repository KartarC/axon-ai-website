How it fits

A clear workflow, from start to finish.

Discover and define

Agree on audience, pages, enquiry fields, integrations and responsibilities. Provide approved capabilities and examples; certifications and results must be supportable.

Design and build

Develop responsive pages, accessible controls and clear content. Custom software gets agreed workflows, permissions and acceptance criteria.

Test and hand over

Review forms, links, mobile behavior and content accuracy. Agree on hosting, domain ownership, support and how future changes will be requested.

Questions about Ovrendi Studio

Do you guarantee search rankings or AI citations?

No. We build useful, accessible content and sound technical foundations. Search engines and AI services decide what to index, rank and cite.

Can you build more than a brochure website?

Yes, custom applications and integrations can be scoped as a Studio project. Features, delivery dates and support are agreed before work begins.

What should I bring to the first discussion?

Your current website, target customers, services, brand assets and one example of a good enquiry. For software, bring a walkthrough of the current process and its main pain points.
