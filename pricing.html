How the trial and billing work

Launch prices are per company, billed monthly in USD. Existing subscriptions retain their agreed price. Canadian card issuers may convert USD to CAD and charge conversion fees.

Start a 14-day trial without a card. To continue automatically, choose a plan in Plan & Billing, accept the recurring billing terms and complete Stripe checkout with a card. Your remaining trial is preserved; Stripe confirms the first charge date. Very short remaining trials may receive a small extension. Without a completed subscription, you will not be charged.

Subscriptions renew monthly until canceled. Use Manage billing to cancel before the next charge. Contact us for plan changes.
