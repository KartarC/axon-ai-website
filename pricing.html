Pricing questions, answered

Can I really add unlimited users for one flat price?

Yes. Every plan includes unlimited team accounts, and operators don't even need accounts — they work from QR codes on any phone. Your price never changes as you hire.

On the Growth plan, which 3 modules should I pick?

Most shops start with Production Board + Job Costing + Shop Traveler — scheduling, margins, and paperless work orders cover the daily pain. You can swap modules anytime, so the first pick isn't a commitment.

What is the 30-day money-back guarantee?

If Ovrendi hasn't earned its keep in your first 30 days on a paid plan, email info@ovrendi.com and we refund that month. No forms, no argument.

Can I upgrade from Starter to Growth or Suite later?

Anytime, from the billing page in the app — the change takes effect immediately and your data carries over untouched. Downgrades work the same way.

Is there a setup fee or implementation cost?

No. Signup is self-serve: create your shop, add your machines with their hourly rates, and you're running — most shops are live inside 10 minutes. There is nothing to implement and nobody to schedule.

Is annual billing available?

Plans bill monthly by default. If you'd prefer annual billing (with two months free), email info@ovrendi.com and we'll set it up.
