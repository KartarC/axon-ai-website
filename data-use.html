Support, activity and analytics data

Updated October 9, 2026.

Bug reports

Reports include the details you submit, your company and user identifiers, a page category and report status. Approved Ovrendi staff use these to investigate issues. Please do not submit passwords, payment information or confidential documents.

Account activity

For customer support and account management, approved staff can see your last sign-in time, last recently used app page category and last activity time. The app sends a heartbeat while its page is visible and you have interacted recently. “Recently active” means a heartbeat was received within five minutes; it is an estimate, not a guarantee that someone is online. We keep the latest activity record, not a full browsing history.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we collect page categories, broad referral categories, form starts and successful form submissions. A random visit identifier groups events in a browser tab; it expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. We do not put names, emails, form contents, query strings or IP addresses in our analytics event table. For abuse prevention, an IP-derived hash limits requests; it changes each minute and is removed by the daily cleanup. Hosting providers may maintain their own operational logs. Raw analytics events are removed after 30 days by the daily cleanup job.

Use Analytics preferences on the website to decline or withdraw permission. Withdrawal stops new events; it does not erase previously collected events. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable optional analytics. Reports and account activity are operational support features, separate from optional website analytics.

Analytics are used to improve Ovrendi, not sold or used for advertising profiles. Contact hello@getovrendi.com about access, corrections or deletion requests.
